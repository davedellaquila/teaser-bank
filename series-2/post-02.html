Anatomy of a buddy

Series 2 · Post 2 of 14 · Proposed for Nov 4, 2026.

The beat: What a buddy actually is: a thread you can talk to, a repo that remembers, a homepage showing current state, an inbox it triages.

So what's a buddy, exactly? Not an app. Not a chatbot window you close and lose. A buddy is four things: a conversation you can talk to, a repo where it keeps everything it learns, a homepage that shows what it's working on right now, and an inbox it triages so nothing lands on me raw. Thread, memory, dashboard, inbox. That's the whole anatomy. Close the chat and nothing evaporates. The buddy still has the file, the history, the next action. It's less 'software' and more 'the new hire who never forgets orientation day.' 🧩
